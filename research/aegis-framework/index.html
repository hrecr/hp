PROTOCOL ASSURANCE · RESEARCH DIRECTION

From protocol descriptions to machine-checkable security obligations.

AEGIS is a research direction for turning post-quantum IoT authentication and key-establishment protocols into structured framework models, a typed DSL, formal security games, and Lean 4 or Coq/Rocq proof obligations.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Security claims need a common language.

01

Fragmented models

Security analyses use different models, adversarial interfaces, and vocabularies.

02

Hard comparisons

Guarantees that share a name may rely on different freshness, corruption, or partnering definitions.

03

IoT constraints

State, energy, packet loss, communication cost, and partial leakage influence real protocol behavior.

04

Post-quantum transition

KEMs and signatures introduce new message sizes, computation costs, negotiation choices, and downgrade concerns.

AEGIS does not replace established AKE models or formal verification tools. It aims to provide a common intermediate representation that makes assumptions, coverage, and missing obligations explicit.
