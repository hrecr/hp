About AEGIS

AEGIS explores how heterogeneous post-quantum IoT authentication and key-establishment protocols can be normalized, checked against a common framework, and translated into machine-checkable proof obligations.

PROJECT SCOPE

A deliberately bounded research direction.

In scope

  • Post-quantum IoT authentication and AKE
  • Protocol extraction and normalization
  • Structural compatibility
  • Typed intermediate specifications
  • Game-based security definitions
  • Lean and Rocq proof obligations
  • Evidence-based reports

Outside current scope

  • Silent protocol repair
  • Automatic invention of complete cryptographic reductions
  • Full UC or quantum-UC claims
  • Implementation side-channel analysis
  • Guarantees beyond the stated model and assumptions
  • Automated vulnerability remediation
