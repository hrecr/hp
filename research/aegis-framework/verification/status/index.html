EVIDENCE VOCABULARY

Verification Result Status

A controlled vocabulary keeps generated statements, completed proofs, assumptions, open obligations, and refutations from collapsing into one misleading score.

EXACT STATUS DEFINITIONS

Every label has one narrow meaning.

Machine-checked

The exact frozen theorem has a complete kernel-accepted proof without prohibited placeholders.

Machine-checked under assumptions

The proof is accepted and depends on explicitly listed assumptions.

Structural certificate verified

A structural property is machine-checked; this is not automatically a complete cryptographic theorem.

Open

The statement is well-formed, but no complete proof is available.

Proof rejected

A submitted proof was rejected; this does not show the theorem is false.

Blocked

Required model information or backend support is missing.

Untrusted placeholder

The apparent proof depends on a placeholder, hidden axiom, or unapproved dependency.

Formally refuted

A checked proof of negation or valid checked counterexample exists.

Not applicable

The property does not apply to the selected configuration.

Disabled

The property was not selected.
